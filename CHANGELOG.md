# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (preview), 2026-11-01

First public preview, for FuriOS on the FuriPhone FLX1s.

- Background recorder that reads the hardware step counter through SensorFW and keeps counting while the interface is closed.
- Today, History and Achievements pages with charts, daily goals, streaks and milestones.
- Distance and energy estimates, pause, and manual correction of today's steps.
- Notifications for goals, encouragement and milestones.
- CSV export and import, import from a Stepsy database, and automatic local copies.
- English, German and Russian interface.
